Kishan Health Sync

A personal, one-user bridge that copies daily wellness data from Google Health to Intervals.icu.

Fitbit Air records heart rate, resting heart rate, HRV, sleep, SpO2, respiratory rate, and steps. Google Health makes those available through its API. This sync reads them and writes them as wellness fields in Intervals.icu. It writes wellness only, no workouts. Wahoo remains the authoritative activity source.

This site exists only as the homepage for the Google Cloud OAuth consent screen for this personal app. It collects no data, stores no data, and has no login.
